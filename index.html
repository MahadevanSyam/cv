// 01 — MISSION BRIEFING

Who's at the controls

I work where electronics meets software. My background is Electronics and Communication Engineering, my day job is making automotive software testing faster and more reliable, and my favourite side quests involve oscillators, sensors and keyboards (the musical kind).

At KPIT I build Python automation and CI pipelines for AUDI (CARIAD) and HONDA programmes, and I've helped build an internal platform that generates test scripts straight from requirements. Before that I interned across ISRO's avionics division, an embedded-sensor startup and C-DAC, and chaired IEEE SPS at VIT for a year.

I like projects where engineering rigour and business sense have to share the same room.
